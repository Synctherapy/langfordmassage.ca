What Sets a BC Registered Massage Therapist (RMT) Apart?

In British Columbia, Registered Massage Therapists are regulated healthcare professionals governed under the College of Complementary Health Professionals of BC (CCHPBC). An RMT utilizes advanced orthopedic assessments, joint biomechanics, and clinical soft-tissue manipulation to deliver measurable pain relief.

  • Objective Orthopedic Testing
    We evaluate active, passive, and resisted ranges of motion to identify the exact muscle groups causing movement restriction.
  • Customized Soft-Tissue Techniques
    Applying evidence-based deep tissue, trigger point therapy, and myofascial mobilization for measurable relief.
  • Prescribed Home-Care Exercises
    Receive functional stretches and ergonomic corrections to support lasting recovery between visits.
