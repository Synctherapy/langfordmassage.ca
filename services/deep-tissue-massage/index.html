CLINICAL TECHNIQUES

Evidence-Based Treatment Methods

Techniques applied during your deep tissue session:

  • Myofascial Release: Sustained traction applied to connective tissue to release fascial adhesions.
  • Trigger Point Therapy: Concentrated ischemic compression on hyperirritable muscle knots.
  • Cross-Fiber Friction: Transverse friction to reorganize collagen fibers in chronically irritated tendons.
  • Joint Mobilization: Gentle movement of joint capsules to enhance synovial fluid circulation.
