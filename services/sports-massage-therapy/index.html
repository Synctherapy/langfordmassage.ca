DUAL CREDENTIAL ADVANTAGE

Integrated RMT & Certified Athletic Therapy

Treatments are delivered by Daryl Stubbs, who holds dual professional credentials as a Registered Massage Therapist (RMT) and a Certified Athletic Therapist (CAT(C)). We don't just massage tight muscles—we analyze joint biomechanics, assess movement patterns, and correct the compensatory imbalances causing athletic breakdown.

  • Pre-Event Preparation
    Brisk, stimulating soft-tissue techniques designed to enhance blood flow and prepare the nervous system for peak output.
  • Post-Event Recovery & Flushing
    Gentle-to-moderate flushing strokes and passive joint mobilization aimed at reducing delayed onset muscle soreness (DOMS). To establish an optimal recovery frequency, review our RMT treatment schedule guide.
  • Overuse Tendon Rehabilitation
    Transverse friction, active release techniques, and functional muscle rebalancing for runner's knee, plantar fasciitis, and IT band friction, frequently combined with targeted myofascial release therapy.
