BENEFITS & OUTCOMES

Why Choose an RMT for Relaxation Massage?

Unlike standard spa massages, our relaxation massage treatments are delivered by a licensed Registered Massage Therapist (RMT) and are eligible for direct billing under your extended health benefits plan:

  • Parasympathetic Nervous System Activation
    Shifts your body out of "fight or flight" mode, helping lower resting heart rate and blood pressure while improving sleep quality.
  • Enhanced Circulation & Lymphatic Flow
    Long, rhythmic Swedish effleurage strokes encourage venous return and accelerate the clearance of metabolic waste products.
  • Gentle Muscular Decompression
    Eases superficial muscle tightness and tension headaches without intense localized pressure. When chronic deeper knots require targeted therapeutic pressure, our deep tissue massage therapy in Langford provides deeper soft-tissue release.
