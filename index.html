QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions About Massage in Langford

Everything you need to know about our Registered Massage Therapy treatments, direct billing, and appointment preparation.

Can I see an RMT in Langford without a doctor's referral? +

Yes. In British Columbia, you do not need a medical doctor's referral to receive treatment from a Registered Massage Therapist. You can book directly online through our Jane App calendar. (Note: A few specific extended health insurance plans may ask for a prescription for claim reimbursement—check your policy handbook for details).

How does direct billing work if I have dual insurance coverage? +

If you are covered under two extended health plans (e.g. your own plan through Pacific Blue Cross and a spouse's plan through Canada Life), we submit to your primary insurer first. Once adjudicated, you receive an official receipt showing the remaining balance, which can then be submitted to the secondary plan.

What is the difference between an RMT and a Certified Athletic Therapist? +

A Registered Massage Therapist (RMT) specializes in soft-tissue manipulation, deep tissue de-escalation, and nervous system down-regulation. A Certified Athletic Therapist (CAT(C)) specializes in rapid injury assessment, biomechanics, and active physical rehabilitation. Daryl Stubbs holds dual credentials in both disciplines.

Where do I park when visiting the Wale Road clinic from Langford? +

We provide dedicated, free ground-level patient parking stalls directly in front of the clinic at 132–328 Wale Rd #120, Colwood. There are no parking meters or long walks.

Do you accept ICBC or WorkSafeBC (WCB) claims? +

No. Our clinic focuses exclusively on private extended health insurance plans and private-pay clients. We do not process ICBC or WorkSafeBC (WCB) claims.
