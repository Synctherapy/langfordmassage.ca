RECOVERY ROADMAP

Recommended Treatment Cadence by Stage

In British Columbia, a licensed Registered Massage Therapist (RMT) creates an individualized treatment plan based on clinical orthopedic assessment rather than recommending a generic schedule:

  • 1. Acute Pain or Recent Muscle Spasm (1–2x Weekly)
    For recent strains, acute spasms, or flare-ups. Frequent visits over 2–3 weeks help break the pain cycle and restore range of motion, often utilizing targeted deep tissue therapy or sports rehabilitation.
  • 2. Chronic Pain & Postural Strain (Every 2–3 Weeks)
    For persistent desk-work neck and shoulder pain or sciatica nerve compression. Allows progressive release while you implement corrective home stretches.
  • 3. Preventative & Athletic Maintenance (Every 4–6 Weeks)
    Monthly check-ins prevent minor muscular tightness from escalating back into chronic holding patterns. Review our transparent treatment rates and clinic hours to plan your schedule.

BC HEALTH REGULATION

What Is the Definition of an RMT in BC?

In British Columbia, a Registered Massage Therapist (RMT) is a primary healthcare provider regulated under the Health Professions Act and the College of Complementary Health Professionals of BC (CCHPBC).

RMTs pass standardized provincial board examinations and maintain strict continuing education, patient confidentiality, and clinical practice standards.
