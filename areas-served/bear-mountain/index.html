How to Reach Our Wale Road Clinic from Bear Mountain

Visiting from Bear Mountain Resort, Country Club Way, or Echo Valley is quick and straightforward:

  • Route 1: Via Millstream & Veterans Memorial Pkwy (9-11 Mins)
    Take Bear Mountain Pkwy down to Millstream Rd. Follow Veterans Memorial Pkwy south to Sooke Rd (BC-14 E), turn left, then turn left onto Wale Rd.

    Head south on Hwy 1, take Exit 10 for Colwood/View Royal (Island Hwy), connect onto Sooke Rd, and turn right into 328 Wale Rd.

    No parking meters or tight ramps. Park free in front of Coastal Offices (Suite #120) behind the Holiday Inn.
