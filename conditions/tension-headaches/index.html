Why Suboccipital Trigger Points Cause "Band of Pressure" Headaches

When working at computers or driving, forward-head posture forces the small muscles at the base of your skull (suboccipitals) to contract constantly to keep your eyes level. This creates radiating referral pain patterns:

  • Suboccipital Muscle Release (C1-C2 Spine)
    Gentle manual traction and ischemic compression at the occipital ridge to decompress the greater occipital nerve, using specialized deep tissue techniques.
  • Upper Trapezius & Sternocleidomastoid (SCM) Deactivation
    Releasing the "coat hanger" tension pattern across the top of the shoulders that refers pain behind the eyes and temples. Also check our neck and shoulder pain guide.
  • Jaw & Masseter Fascial Balancing
    Stress clenching magnifies temple headaches. We integrate gentle cranial and TMJ myofascial release when jaw clenching accompanies headache episodes.
