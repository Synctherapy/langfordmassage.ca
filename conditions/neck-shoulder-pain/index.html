BIOMECHANICAL REALIGNMENT

Treating the Root Cause of "Desk-Worker" Neck Lock

When you lean forward toward computer monitors or phones, your head exerts heavy mechanical load on your cervical spine. Our registered massage therapists use precision clinical techniques to release the holding patterns:

  • Suboccipital Decompression
    Gentle traction and trigger point release on the suboccipital muscles at the base of the skull to deactivate cervicogenic headache referral pain.
  • Levator Scapulae & Upper Trapezius Release
    Deep ischemic compression on stubborn knots between the neck and shoulder blade to restore full side-to-side rotation.
  • Pectoral & Anterior Chest Opening
    Myofascial release on pectoralis minor to pull rounded shoulders backward into natural postural alignment.
